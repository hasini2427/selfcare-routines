The Beginner Home Workout Week

No equipment · 15 to 25 minutes · Start slowly
01

Warm up (3 to 5 minutes)

March on the spot, arm circles, hip circles and gentle squats. Warm muscles move better and feel safer.

Every dayDo not skip
02

Lower body (Mon, Thu)

3 rounds of 10 to 12 squats, 10 reverse lunges per leg and 12 glute bridges. Rest 30 to 45 seconds between rounds.

Legs and glutesNo equipment
03

Upper body and core (Tue, Fri)

3 rounds of 6 to 10 push-ups (on your knees is fine), 20 second planks and 10 slow dead bugs per side.

Arms and coreGo at your pace
04

Cardio (Wed, Sat)

Alternate 30 seconds of brisk marching or low-impact jumping jacks with 30 seconds of rest, for 10 to 15 minutes.

Heart rate upLow impact
05

Stretch and rest (Sun)

A gentle full-body stretch, a walk and plenty of water. Rest days are when your body recovers.

RecoveryMobility
Why it works

Consistency over intensity

  • Fits in a small hostel room with a mat or towel
  • Short sessions are easier to repeat every week
  • Mixes strength, cardio and stretching so you do not get bored
Handy to have

What you'll need

A yoga mat or a folded blanket
Comfortable shoes and clothes
A water bottle
A phone timer
